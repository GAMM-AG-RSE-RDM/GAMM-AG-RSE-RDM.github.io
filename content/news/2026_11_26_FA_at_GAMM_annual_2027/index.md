The abstract submission deadline for the joint [GAMM and DMV annual Meeting in
Ulm](https://jahrestagung.gamm.org/joint-annual-meeting-2027/joint-annual-meeting/)
is only 6 days away. If you want to relate your contribution to our
activity group, you can join our dedicated Session in S22 by leaving a
comment pointing this out in the additional information about your
submission.

Also, the activity group spokespersons have successfully applied for
__MS 1 Benchmarking in applied mathematics and mechanics__, featuring 6
contributions on various topics around benchmarking in development and
practice, on your notebook or your HPC cluster, supporting tools,
providing or preserving structures.

And most importantly, we will have stall at the exhibitors area
(hopefully close to the [MaRDI](mardi4nfdi.de) stall) that needs your
input. We will feature a __Software and Data Marketplace__ that give
_you_ the chance to _feature your favorite software or data project_
in one of the coffee or lunch breaks. So please contact us, either in
person on [our annual workshop next week in
Cologne](https://events.hifis.net/event/3698/timetable/#all), or by
[email](mailto:gamm-rse+rdm-chairs@listserv.dfn.de?subject=GAMM%202027%20Stall%20Contribution)
to the activity group chairs, and let us know what you plan to
present.
