📢 The next annual meeting of the GAMM Activity Group on Research Software Engineering and Research Data Management in Mathematics & Mechanics will take place on December 1-2, 2026, at the [Center for Data and Simulation Sciences](https://cds.uni-koeln.de/) at Universität zu Köln. If you are interested in math, mechanics, RSE, RDM, and/or some wild mixture of this, the activity group and this annual meeting is a great place to exchange thoughts!

Join us for this meeting [__here__](https://events.hifis.net/event/3698/), with or without a talk. Both abstract submission and registration are now open! And no, you won't be alone; we have exciting RSE & RDM keynotes coming up from [Katrin Schöning-Stierand](https://www.hcds.uni-hamburg.de/hcds/team/katrin-schoening-stierand.html) (University of Hamburg) and [Matthias Lieber](https://wwwpub.zih.tu-dresden.de/~mlieber/) (TU Dresden)!

If you are interested in the activity group but cannot attend the meeting, you can join our [_mailing list_](https://gamm-ag-rse-rdm.github.io/) to stay in touch.

P.S.: Yes, we had to change the dates due to multiple unforeseen circumstances. Very sorry for the confusion.
